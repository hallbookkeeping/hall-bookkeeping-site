Bookkeeper in Mount Pleasant, SC

Mount Pleasant runs on small operators: contractors and remodelers working off Highway 41, restaurants and shops on Coleman Boulevard and Shem Creek, and the service businesses that follow a town growing this fast. I keep books for exactly that kind of business, and I am fifteen minutes over the bridge.

What I handle

Monthly bookkeeping on QuickBooks Online: every transaction categorized, every bank and card account reconciled against the statement, and your profit and loss and balance sheet in your inbox by the 15th business day. You get a fixed monthly fee, set on our first call, with no contract behind it.

If the books are behind, I do the catch-up first. That is a separate flat quote covering however many months need rebuilding, and it does not change once you have it in writing. When it is done, your CPA gets a tax ready file and the monthly work starts from clean numbers.

How working together looks

Almost everything happens inside QuickBooks Online and email, so you are not printing anything or dropping papers off. You add me as the accountant user on your file, which gives me your books and nothing else. Questions get answered the same day. When a face to face makes sense, I am local, and coffee on Coleman is an easy meeting.

What it costs

Monthly service runs $400 to $750 depending on how many accounts you run and how many transactions move through them. A typical Mount Pleasant business with two or three accounts and moderate volume lands near the bottom of that range. You get your exact number on a 15 minute call, in writing, the same day.
