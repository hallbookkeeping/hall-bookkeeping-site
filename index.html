Frequently asked questions

Do you file my taxes?

No. Tax filing goes to a CPA. When it is time to file, I hand your CPA clean, tax ready books.

Do you run payroll?

No. I track payroll in your books but do not process it.

Do I need QuickBooks already?

No. If you already use QuickBooks Online, I work in your existing account. If you do not, I set it up for you when we start.

How much does it cost?

Monthly service runs $400 to $1,000 based on your accounts and transaction count. Cleanup is a flat quote set before any work starts. Both are fixed numbers you see up front.

How do we get started?

Schedule a call. If it is a fit, you invite me as a read-only accountant user on your QuickBooks and I take it from there.

Are you a CPA?

No. I am a bookkeeper. I keep your books clean, reconciled, and tax ready. A CPA files your taxes, and I work directly with yours when it is time.
