About me

I'm Taylor Hall, the owner of Hall Bookkeeping. I was born and raised in Charleston and graduated from the College of Charleston with a degree in Finance.

Before starting Hall Bookkeeping, I worked as a financial analyst at a business brokerage firm, where I spent three years reading the financials of small companies being bought and sold.

I started Hall Bookkeeping to provide small business owners with reliable, straightforward bookkeeping. I handle your books, reconcile your accounts each month, and keep your financials accurate and up to date.

Frequently asked questions

Do you file my taxes?

No. Tax filing goes to a CPA. When it is time to file, I hand your CPA clean, tax ready books.

Do I need QuickBooks already?

No. If you already use QuickBooks Online, I work in your existing account. If you do not, I set it up for you when we start.

How much does it cost?

Monthly service runs $400 to $750 based on your accounts and transaction count. Cleanup is a flat quote set before any work starts. Both are fixed numbers you see up front.

What happens in the first month?

I review your existing books, tell you what needs correcting before we start monthly work, and quote the cleanup separately if there is one. Then monthly service begins.

Are you a CPA?

No. I am a bookkeeper. I keep your books clean, reconciled, and tax ready. A CPA files your taxes, and I work directly with yours when it is time.
